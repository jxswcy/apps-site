NuTTS Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

NuTTS is designed as a local document-to-speech tool. Your imported documents, generated audio files, and provider settings stay on your device unless you choose to use a configured speech provider.

Documents and generated audio

NuTTS stores imported document text, document metadata, synthesis status, and generated audio files locally on your device so you can manage and replay them inside the app.

API keys

If you configure Alibaba Cloud Model Studio or a compatible Qwen-TTS endpoint, your API key is stored in the system Keychain. NuTTS does not hard-code or operate a shared API key.

Speech synthesis requests

When you generate audio, the text needed for synthesis, selected model, voice, and instruction may be sent from your device to the provider you configured. That provider's privacy policy and terms apply to its processing.

File access and playback

NuTTS uses system document import permissions to read the files you choose. It may use local storage and audio playback capabilities to download, save, play, export, and continue playback for generated audio.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

NuTTS does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies. NuTTS does not sell personal data.

Support and changes

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide is used only to respond to your request and improve the app. This policy may be updated as NuTTS changes.

Privacy at a glance

NuTTS keeps the document library and generated audio on device, while synthesis uses the provider you configure.

  • Documents and audio are stored locally.
  • API keys are stored in Keychain.
  • Synthesis text is sent only when you generate audio.
  • No ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking.
