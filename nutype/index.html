A quieter way to type without typing.

Shortcut dictation

Hold your shortcut, speak naturally, and release to send cleaned text into the app you are already using.

Cleaned text

Remove filler words, smooth phrasing, and add natural punctuation before text is inserted.

Insert anywhere

NuType is built for current-input insertion, so notes, chats, documents, and search fields stay in flow.

Flexible providers

Use local or user-configured speech and AI providers, with keys stored locally on your Mac.

Local-first privacy

Dictation starts only when you trigger it. History, dictionary, and provider settings stay under your control.

Built for private speech and text.

NuType is designed around local-first voice input. It records only when you start dictation, stores sensitive credentials in Keychain, and does not run ads or cross-app tracking.

User-triggered recording

Microphone access is used only while you are dictating.

Your providers

If you configure an external speech or AI provider, requests go from your device to that provider.

No tracking

NuType does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.
