NuFile Privacy Policy

NuFile is designed for local document workflows. Your selected OFD/PDF files are processed on your device.

Effective date: May 13, 2026

Local document processing

NuFile opens, previews, converts, watermarks, and exports documents locally on your device. NuFile does not upload your OFD/PDF files to NuFile servers for processing.

Files and folders you select

NuFile accesses only the files you import and the output folders you choose. On platforms that require persistent access, NuFile may store a local security-scoped bookmark for your selected output folder.

No ads, analytics, or tracking

NuFile does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.

Purchases

NuFile Pro purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. NuFile does not receive or store your payment card information.

Support

If you contact support, any information you choose to provide will be used only to respond to your request and improve the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated when NuFile changes. Material updates will be reflected on this page.
