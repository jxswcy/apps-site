NuBarCodePrivacy Policy

NuBarCode is designed for local barcode scanning, importing, and QR code generation. Your code content is processed on your device.

Local barcode processing

NuBarCode scans camera frames, selected photos, selected files, screenshots, and PDF pages on your device. NuBarCode does not upload scan results, generated QR codes, or imported files to NuBarCode servers.

Camera, photos, files, and screenshots

NuBarCode requests camera access for live scanning, photo access for images you choose, and file access for images or PDFs you import. Generated QR images are saved only when you choose to save or export them.

No ads, analytics, or tracking

NuBarCode does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking technologies.

Support

If you contact support at jxswcy@gmail.com, the information you choose to provide is used only to respond to your request and improve the app.

Privacy at a glance

NuBarCode works locally with the camera, photos, files, screenshots, and barcode content you choose.

  • Barcode content is processed on device.
  • Camera, photos, files, and screenshots are used only for features you start.
  • No ads, analytics, or cross-app tracking.
