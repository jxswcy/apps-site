NuBarCodeSupport

Help for camera scanning, image and PDF import, barcode recognition, QR code generation, and result handling.

Scan with the camera

Open the Scan tab and point the camera at a barcode. If the camera is unavailable, check camera permission in system settings and make sure no other app is using it.

Import images, PDFs, or screenshots

Use the photo or file import action to recognize barcodes from selected images, screenshots, or PDF pages. If nothing is found, try a clearer image with the full code visible.

Generate and save QR codes

Open the Generate tab, enter text or a URL, then save the generated QR image to Photos or Files when you need to share it elsewhere.

Read and inspect results

Recognized content can be copied. Links can be opened directly. Use ASCII and HEX views when you need to inspect invisible characters or raw bytes.

Contact support

If you need help, email jxswcy@gmail.com. Include your device, system version, whether you used camera/import/generation, the barcode format if known, and a short description of what happened.

Quick help

Most scan issues come from camera permission, blurry images, cropped codes, low contrast, or an unsupported barcode format. For support, email jxswcy@gmail.com.
