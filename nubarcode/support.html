NuBarCode Support

Help for camera scanning, image and PDF import, QR code generation, and result handling.

Scan with the camera

Open the Scan tab and point the camera at a QR code. If the camera is unavailable, check camera permission in system settings and make sure no other app is using it.

Import images or PDFs

Use the photo or file import action to recognize QR codes from selected images or PDF pages. If nothing is found, try a clearer image with the full QR code visible.

Generate and save QR codes

Open the Generate tab, enter text or a URL, then save the generated QR image to Photos or Files when you need to share it elsewhere.

Read and copy results

Recognized content can be copied. Links can be opened directly. Use the text and hex views when you need to inspect invisible characters or raw bytes.

Contact support

If you need help, include your device, system version, whether you used camera/import/generation, and a short description of what happened.

Quick help

Most scan issues come from camera permission, blurry images, cropped QR codes, or low contrast.
