NuIPTVPrivacy Policy

NuIPTV works with IPTV sources you provide. The app does not provide, host, or recommend IPTV channels.

User-provided IPTV sources

NuIPTV stores source names, source URLs, refresh status, channel groups, channel metadata, favorites, and recent playback records so you can manage and play the sources you add.

Playback, EPG, and network requests

When you import, refresh, or play a source, NuIPTV contacts the IPTV or EPG servers you configured. Stream availability, server logs, and provider handling are controlled by those services.

iCloud sync

If enabled, NuIPTV uses your iCloud account to sync supported library data such as sources, favorites, recent playback, EPG configuration, and selected settings across your devices.

AI subtitles and API keys

Optional AI subtitle features use your own compatible provider configuration. API keys are stored in the system Keychain. When enabled, necessary audio or text may be sent to the provider you configured.

Purchases

NuIPTV Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. NuIPTV uses StoreKit entitlement information to unlock paid features.

Ads, analytics, and tracking

NuIPTV does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. NuIPTV does not sell personal data.

Privacy at a glance

NuIPTV stores your library on device, can sync selected data with iCloud, and contacts only the services you configure or play.

  • No built-in IPTV content sources.
  • API keys are stored in Keychain.
  • AI requests use the endpoint you configure.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs.
