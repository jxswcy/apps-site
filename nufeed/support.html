NuFeed Support

Help for subscriptions, Pro features, AI configuration, and iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Common questions

RSS reading works without a Pro purchase. Web subscriptions, full article extraction, bilingual translation, and full-article summaries require NuFeed Pro. iCloud sync uses your own iCloud Drive.

AI configuration

AI features require your own AI provider API key. NuFeed does not include built-in AI service credits, and requests are sent only to the provider you configure.

Contact

If you need help, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page. Include your device model, system version, NuFeed version, and a short description of what happened.

Quick help

When reporting an issue, include your device model, system version, NuFeed version, and the steps that led to the problem.
