NuFeed Support

Need help with NuFeed? This page covers common support topics for the iPhone, iPad, and Mac versions.

Common questions

  • RSS reading works without a Pro purchase.
  • Web subscriptions, full article extraction, bilingual translation, and full-article summaries require NuFeed Pro.
  • AI features require your own AI provider API key. NuFeed does not include built-in AI service credits.
  • iCloud sync uses your own iCloud Drive and does not rely on a NuFeed-operated server.

Contact

If you need help, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

When reporting an issue, please include your device model, system version, NuFeed version, and a short description of what happened.
