Common questions

  • RSS reading works without a Pro purchase.
  • Web subscriptions, full article extraction, bilingual translation, and full-article summaries require NuFeed Pro.
  • iCloud sync uses your own iCloud Drive and does not rely on a NuFeed-operated server.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

AI configuration

AI features require your own AI provider API key. NuFeed does not include built-in AI service credits, and requests are sent only to the provider you configure.

Contact

If you need help, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

When reporting an issue, include your device model, system version, NuFeed version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Support requests are used only to reply and improve NuFeed.
