NuFeedPrivacy Policy

NuFeed is designed as a local-first reader. This policy explains what the app stores, when data syncs, and how optional AI features work.

Information stored by NuFeed

NuFeed stores your subscriptions, folders, articles, reading state, favorite state, extraction settings, and app preferences on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, NuFeed stores sync data in your own iCloud Drive so your devices can share subscriptions and reading progress.

AI API keys and AI requests

NuFeed does not provide a built-in AI service account. If you use bilingual translation or full-article summaries, you configure your own third-party AI provider and API key. When you use AI features, relevant article text may be sent to the provider you configured.

iCloud sync

If enabled, NuFeed uses your iCloud Drive to sync app data such as subscriptions, article state, read/unread state, favorites, and related metadata. NuFeed does not operate a separate sync server.

Purchases

NuFeed Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, while NuFeed receives entitlement information from StoreKit to unlock paid features.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

NuFeed does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. NuFeed does not sell personal data.

Support and policy changes

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide is used only to respond to your request and improve the app. This policy may be updated when NuFeed changes.

Privacy at a glance

NuFeed keeps the core reading experience on your devices and uses user-controlled services for sync and AI features.

  • No NuFeed-operated sync server.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs.
  • AI requests use the provider you configure.
